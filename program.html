Program

Tanvika's arangetram is arranged as a traditional Bharathanatyam margam, moving from invocation and pure rhythm into expressive storytelling shaped by
Shakthi - The Power Within.

01

Pushpanjali & Saraswathi

Pushpanjali

Ragam Saraswathi Thalam Adi Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

A traditional devotional opening item in Bharatanatyam. It serves as an invocation piece asking for the blessings of Goddess Saraswathi—the deity of knowledge, music, arts, wisdom, and learning—before beginning a performance or practice session.

Saraswathi Kouthvam

Ragam Saraswathi Thalam Chaturashra Eka (Traditional Kouthvam rhythm structure) Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

A structured rhythmic hymn (Kouthvam) dedicated to Goddess Saraswathi. It weaves together complex rhythmic syllables (jatis) with poetic descriptions praising the Goddess's pure white attire, her Veena, and her ability to dispel the darkness of ignorance.

02

Jathiswaram

Ragam Saveri Thalam Rupakam Choreography Kalakshetra

A vibrant example of Nrtta, or pure technical dance, featuring no lyrics or storytelling. Instead, the dancer interweaves complex rhythmic patterns, jathis, with melodic vocal notes, swaras. Set to the beautiful, classic Saveri ragam, it demands incredible precision, stamina, and seamless spatial transitions.

03

Hanuman Shabdam

Ragam Ragamalika Thalam Misra Chapu Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

A narrative piece that serves as a bridge into the art of Abhinaya, or expression. Through rhythmic intervals and expressive poetry, the dancer brings to life the unwavering devotion, immense strength, and heroic exploits of Lord Hanuman, showcasing his deep surrender at the feet of Lord Rama.

04

Varnam (Dasaratha Nandana)

Ragam Ragamalika Thalam Adi Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

This spectacular Varnam brings to life the complete journey of Lord Rama, translating the timeless verses of the Ramayana into the expressive language of dance and rhythm. It is a magnificent, visual chronicle of a life defined by Dharma, or righteousness, taking the audience on an unforgettable journey from a tender infancy to supreme kingship.

Interval

05

Rama Gaava

Ragam Mand Thalam Adi Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

Shifting into the celebratory mood of a traditional Bhajan, this piece fills the auditorium with joyous, soulful devotion. The lively lyrics invite the entire congregation to join in singing the praises of Lord Rama, capturing the sweet energy of unconditional love, or bhakti.

06

Katama Rayuda (Betrayi Sami Devuda)

Ragam Traditional Folk tune Thalam Tisra nadai Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

A lively, popular Telugu composition that celebrates a deeply protective, localized avatar, Kadiri Narasimha, of the divine. Blending semi-classical folk steps with expressive, spirited storytelling of the dashavatharas, it brings a cheerful, energetic bounce to the stage that highlights the playful, celebratory side of classical expression.

07

Navarasa Rama Keertanam

Ragam Ragamalika Thalam Adi Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

A tour de force of dramatic storytelling. This brilliant composition traces the epic story of the Ramayana by showcasing the Navarasas, the nine fundamental human emotions, manifested through the life of Lord Rama. The dancer instantly shifts facial expressions and body language to portray love, mirth, compassion, anger, valor, fear, disgust, wonder, and ultimate tranquility.

08

Thillana

Ragam Sindu Bhairavi Thalam Adi Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

The joyous, fast-paced climax of the dance recital. Thillana in Bharatanatyam is a vibrant, rhythmic, and joyful finale piece that showcases the dancer's precision, energy, and expressive brilliance. This specific composition concludes with a beautiful lyrical salute dedicated to the virtues of Lord Rama.

09

Mangalam

Ragam Madhyamavati / Saurashtram Thalam Adi Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

The formal concluding piece of the dance line-up. The dancer performs a final gesture of deep gratitude, seeking forgiveness for any unintended errors during the performance and offering blessings of peace, prosperity, and harmony to the musicians, the Guru, the audience, and the universe.
