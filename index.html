The Guru

Smt. Chandna Talluri

The Guiding Light

Tanvika's artistic foundation has been shaped by the guidance, patience, and vision of her Guru. Under Smt. Chandna Talluri's care, Tanvika has developed the discipline, strength, expression, and reverence that Bharathanatyam asks of every student.

Guru Smt. Chandna's teaching has helped Tanvika understand dance as more than movement. It is prayer, storytelling, rhythm, and a lifelong commitment to learning with humility.

A Note of Gratitude

Guru Brahma Gurur Vishnu Guru Devo Maheshwaraha
Guru Saakshat Para Brahma Tasmai Sree Gurave Namaha

Guru is verily the representative of Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva. She creates, sustains knowledge, and destroys the weeds of ignorance.

We offer our heartfelt pranamams to Smt. Chandna for her tireless dedication, patience, and guidance. It is through her vision that Tanvika's artistic journey has blossomed into today's beautiful presentation. Thank you for igniting the light of art and devotion.

The Ensemble

The Artists

Melody, Rhythm, and Devotion

Ramayaniyam is carried by the conversation between voice, violin, flute, mrudangam, and Tanvika's abhinaya. Meet the artists shaping the devotional world of Rama, Sita, Hanuman, and dharma through melody and rhythm.

Violin

Anuradha Sridhar

A longtime Bay Area ambassador of Carnatic music, rooted in the Lalgudi musical lineage.

Anuradha Sridhar, violinist, vocalist, and composer, has been an active ambassador of Carnatic music in the Bay Area for the past four decades. Her family of legendary musicians traces its musical roots to Saint Tyagaraja through her great-great-grandfather, Lalgudi Ramayyar, a direct disciple of the saint.

Anu was trained in music by her mother, Lalgudi Srimathi Brahmanandam, the ingenious violinist and sister of the legendary Lalgudi G. Jayaraman. As a soloist, Anu has performed with her guru in prestigious venues around the world. As a reputed accompanist, she has accompanied many stalwart Carnatic musicians in concerts over the years.

She is deeply involved in promoting Carnatic music and has volunteered her artistry for community fundraiser shows supporting organizations such as Asha, Sankara Eye Foundation, Sankara Nethralaya, SwayamKrushi, ISKCON, Livermore Temple, Chinmaya Mission, and Sewa International. She has also collaborated with musicians from other genres in the Pan Asian Music Festival hosted by Stanford University and San Francisco World Music.

Anu has choreographed and performed Vadhya Vrindha, a musical ensemble of South Indian classical instruments involving many Bay Area artists. She is an A Top artist of Indian Doordarshan and has been recognized by leading organizations including the Madras Music Academy. She founded Trinity Center for Music in the Bay Area in 1989 and continues to nurture young performers.

Mrudangam

Keshavan Ramamurthy

A versatile percussionist from a four-generation musical family, known for rhythmic depth and dance collaboration.

Thanjavur Ramamurthy Kesavan is a renowned percussionist and Nattuvangam artist in the field of performing arts. He comes from an illustrious lineage and belongs to a family dedicated to music for four generations.

Kesavan began learning Mrudangam at the age of six. By the age of ten, he was accompanying his father, Ramamurthy Bhagavathar, a Harikatha exponent. He also accompanied the renowned spiritual guru Thirumuruga Kirupanandha Variyar.

Over the years, Kesavan has become an expert in several percussion instruments including Thavil, Kanjeera, Pakhawaj, and Ghatam. His recognition as a Mrudangam and Nattuvangam artist comes from deep technical command, musical instinct, and humility in collaboration.

He is an empanelled artist of ICCR and a B High graded artist in All India Radio and Doordarshan. Kesavan has trained and promoted many students, conducted Mrudangam arangetrams, and composed new Alarippu, Pushpanjali, Jatiswaram, Tillana, and many varieties of jathis in different talams for classical dance productions.

Flute

Srivathsa Pasumarthi

A flautist from a family of flute makers and musicians, bringing melodic clarity and rhythmic insight.

Srivathsa Pasumarthi is a seventh-generation flautist from a family of esteemed flutists and flute makers.

His musical journey began under the guidance of his father, Pasumarthi Venkata Ramana. He later continued his training under renowned flautist Shri J. B. Shruthi Sagar of Chennai, with whom he has studied for over a decade.

Srivathsa brings a strong classical foundation, sensitive melodic phrasing, and a deep understanding of rhythm to the ensemble. He is also an A-graded Mridangam artist of All India Radio, Chennai.

Vocal

Shriya Anand

A vocalist and violinist trained under Guru Smt. Anuradha Sridhar for around 17 years.

Shriya Anand is a disciple of Guru Smt. Anuradha Sridhar and has been under her tutelage for around 17 years, learning both vocal and violin.

She has given several vocal and violin solo and duet concerts in the United States and India, and has accompanied several upcoming artists and music schools on violin.

Shriya has composed music and accompanied on violin for multiple dance productions for independent artists and schools across the nation. She has also been a lead vocalist in many productions by Trinity Center for Music in the United States and India.

She has a masters in Clinical Psychology from Columbia University and is currently a researcher at the University of Michigan.

Bharathanatyam Arangetram

Program

Tanvika's arangetram is arranged as a traditional Bharathanatyam margam, moving from invocation and pure rhythm into expressive storytelling shaped by Ramayaniyam - The Path of Dharma.

01

Mallari & Sita Kauthuvam

Mallari

Ragam Gambheera Nattai · Thalam Adi · Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

A grand, traditional invocation piece rooted in South Indian temple processions. Originally played on the Nadaswaram as the deity emerged from the sanctum sanctorum, Mallari features powerful geometric lines and crisp footwork executed in progressive speeds. In today's choreography, the sacred temple courtyard comes alive on stage as Tanvika welcomes the grand, royal procession of Lord Rama and Goddess Sita.

Sita Kauthuvam

Ragam Vaasanthi · Thalam Mishra Chapu · Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

A classical hymn of praise celebrating the virtues, grace, and divine beauty of Goddess Sita. Combining energetic rhythmic syllables with expressive narrative elements, or abhinaya, this piece depicts the inner strength and absolute devotion of Sita, honoring her as the epitome of grace.

02

Jathiswaram

Ragam Saveri · Thalam Rupakam · Choreography Kalakshetra

A vibrant example of Nrtta, or pure technical dance, featuring no lyrics or storytelling. Instead, the dancer interweaves complex rhythmic patterns, jathis, with melodic vocal notes, swaras. Set to the beautiful, classic Saveri ragam, it demands incredible precision, stamina, and seamless spatial transitions.

03

Hanuman Shabdam

Ragam Ragamalika · Thalam Misra Chapu · Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

A narrative piece that serves as a bridge into the art of Abhinaya, or expression. Through rhythmic intervals and expressive poetry, the dancer brings to life the unwavering devotion, immense strength, and heroic exploits of Lord Hanuman, showcasing his deep surrender at the feet of Lord Rama.

04

Varnam (Dasaratha Nandana)

Ragam Ragamalika · Thalam Adi · Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

This spectacular Varnam brings to life the complete journey of Lord Rama, translating the timeless verses of the Ramayana into the expressive language of dance and rhythm. It is a magnificent, visual chronicle of a life defined by Dharma, or righteousness, taking the audience on an unforgettable journey from a tender infancy to supreme kingship.

Interval

05

Rama Gaava

Ragam Mand · Thalam Adi · Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

Shifting into the celebratory mood of a traditional Bhajan, this piece fills the auditorium with joyous, soulful devotion. The lively lyrics invite the entire congregation to join in singing the praises of Lord Rama, capturing the sweet energy of unconditional love, or bhakti.

06

Katama Rayuda (Betrayi Sami Devuda)

Ragam Traditional Folk tune · Thalam Tisra nadai · Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

A lively, popular Telugu composition that celebrates a deeply protective, localized avatar, Kadiri Narasimha, of the divine. Blending semi-classical folk steps with expressive, spirited storytelling of the dashavatharas, it brings a cheerful, energetic bounce to the stage that highlights the playful, celebratory side of classical expression.

07

Navarasa Rama Keertanam

Ragam Ragamalika · Thalam Adi · Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

A tour de force of dramatic storytelling. This brilliant composition traces the epic story of the Ramayana by showcasing the Navarasas, the nine fundamental human emotions, manifested through the life of Lord Rama. The dancer instantly shifts facial expressions and body language to portray love, mirth, compassion, anger, valor, fear, disgust, wonder, and ultimate tranquility.

08

Thillana

Ragam Sindu Bhairavi · Thalam Adi · Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

The joyous, fast-paced climax of the dance recital. Thillana in Bharatanatyam is a vibrant, rhythmic, and joyful finale piece that showcases the dancer's precision, energy, and expressive brilliance. This specific composition concludes with a beautiful lyrical salute dedicated to the virtues of Lord Rama.

09

Mangalam

Ragam Madhyamavati / Saurashtram · Thalam Adi · Choreography Guru Smt. Chandna Talluri

The formal concluding piece of the dance line-up. The dancer performs a final gesture of deep gratitude, seeking forgiveness for any unintended errors during the performance and offering blessings of peace, prosperity, and harmony to the musicians, the Guru, the audience, and the universe.
