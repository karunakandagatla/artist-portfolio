The Journey

Tanvika's journey in Bharatanatyam is a testament to passion, dedication, and an innate love for the arts. Her love affair with dance began at the tender age of five, sparked by a natural inclination toward music and movement. Long before her formal training, Tanvika's artistic spirit shone through as she joyfully practiced steps by the mirror, completely captivated by the rhythm and expression of dance.

She officially began her foundational training under the guidance of Guru Deepa Krishnan, where she built a strong technical base and nurtured her deep appreciation for classical art. In early 2023, Tanvika took the next significant step in her artistic evolution by joining Guru Chandana, under whose expert tutelage she refined her abhinaya (expression), stamina, and intricate nritta (pure dance).

Through years of unwavering commitment, early practice sessions, and countless hours in the dance studio, Tanvika has blossomed into a graceful and poised classical dancer. Her Arangetram marks a magnificent milestone in this lifelong journey—a celebration of hard work, artistic growth, and a profound devotion to the divine art of Bharatanatyam.

Training & Growth

Tanvika's artistic growth has been shaped by a rich blend of rigorous training and diverse performance experiences under the guidance of both Guru Deepa Krishnan and Guru Chandana. Benefiting from the distinct insights and expertise of her gurus, she has continuously expanded her technical precision, stamina, and emotional depth in storytelling.

Beyond the studio, the stage has been Tanvika's greatest classroom. Her vibrant dance journey has taken her across continents, allowing her to share her art on prestigious and culturally rich platforms. Among her most cherished milestones are unforgettable performances in:

  • Ayodhya, India: A deeply spiritual and momentous experience performing in the historic heart of India.
  • Sri Lanka: Showcasing her dedication and grace on an international stage.
  • Local Temples across California: Connecting deeply with community roots through regular and devotional performances close to home.

Each stage has added a unique layer to Tanvika's artistry, transforming her into a confident, versatile performer ready to embrace the sacred milestone of her Arangetram.

Shakthi

The Power Within

Rooted in ancient mythology yet profoundly relevant today, “Shakthi: The Power Within” is a thematic exploration of the multifaceted dimensions of Shakthi—the supreme, all-encompassing cosmic energy that creates, nurtures, and transforms the universe.

Through the intricate footwork of nritta, the expressive storytelling of abhinaya, and the soul-stirring rhythms of classical music, this presentation brings to life the many faces of the feminine divine: the gentle protector, the fierce destroyer of ignorance, and the infinite source of inner strength, grace, and resilience.

Beyond classical storytelling, Shakthi represents a journey of self-discovery and the awakening of the divine spark that resides within every individual. It is a celebration of the inner power, discipline, and resilience that fuel both artistic expression and the human spirit.
