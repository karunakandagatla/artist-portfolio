Honoring Smt. Chandna Talluri, the guiding light behind Tanvika's artistic journey.

Saturday, September 5, 2026 · 2:30 PM PDT

The Guru

Smt. Chandna Talluri

The Guiding Light

Tanvika's artistic foundation has been shaped by the guidance, patience, and vision of her Guru. Under Smt. Chandna Talluri's care, Tanvika has developed the discipline, strength, expression, and reverence that Bharathanatyam asks of every student.

Guru Smt. Chandna's teaching has helped Tanvika understand dance as more than movement. It is prayer, storytelling, rhythm, and a lifelong commitment to learning with humility.

A Note of Gratitude

Guru Brahma Gurur Vishnu Guru Devo Maheshwaraha
Guru Saakshat Para Brahma Tasmai Sree Gurave Namaha

Guru is verily the representative of Brahma, Vishnu, and Shiva. She creates, sustains knowledge, and destroys the weeds of ignorance.

We offer our heartfelt pranamams to Smt. Chandna for her tireless dedication, patience, and guidance. It is through her vision that Tanvika's artistic journey has blossomed into today's beautiful presentation. Thank you for igniting the light of art and devotion.
